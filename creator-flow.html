作者之路
The Creator Journey

从买积分到每月结款 —— 在 ReelDrama 做剧，一共九步。
From credit pack to monthly payout, in nine steps.

1

免费注册Sign up free

作者不需要任何会员。注册即开你的创作工作室。No membership for creators — your studio opens the moment you sign up.

↓
2

买积分包Buy a credit pack

积分预付，按需使用；平台从不垫资，你也不必预付制作费以外的任何东西。Prepaid credits, spent only when you create.

↓
3

开新剧 · 选角Start a series & cast

从公共演员货架挑选面孔 —— 14 位演员，古装、民国、现代、韩剧风一应俱全，人脸全程锁定不漂移。Pick faces from the public actor shelf — 14 actors, consistent across every episode.

↓
4

锁定剧本Lock the script

AI 剧本助手出稿，你逐集审定确认后才进入生成 —— 先锁剧本，再花积分。The script assistant drafts; nothing is generated until you approve.

↓
5

点生成Generate

按集扣积分，生成成功才收费；生成失败不扣一分。Credits are charged per episode — only on success. Failed generations are free.

↓
6

审核 · 发布Review & publish

通过自动审核，剧集即在 App 内上线。Automated moderation, then your series goes live in the app.

↓
7

观众观看Viewers watch

前几集免费看；粉丝可以按你自己定的价格订阅你。First episodes are free. Fans can subscribe to you — at your own price.

↓
8

收入自动记账Earnings recorded automatically

每一笔订阅收入自动记入你的账本；付费收入 100% 归你，仅扣除应用商店自身的抽成。Every sale is recorded automatically. You keep 100% after the app stores' own cut — the platform takes 0%.

↓
9

每月结款Monthly payout

满 $50 起，每月经 PayPal 或 Zelle 打到你登记的账户。Paid monthly via PayPal or Zelle, from a $50 threshold.

↺ 再投制作 · Make more episodes

赚到的钱回到第 2 / 5 步 —— 再买积分、再出新集。剧越拍越多，观众越攒越厚。
Earnings roll back into credits and new episodes. The catalogue compounds.

作者拥有自己作品的全部版权，平台不占份额。
Creators keep their IP — always. Creator inquiries: info@annizon.com
