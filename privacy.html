Privacy Policy — Reel Drama AI

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Reel Drama AI ("we", "the app") is a short-drama app where users watch AI-generated series and creators make their own. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account we store your email address, display name, and a securely hashed password. Accounts let you sync your library, membership, and creator studio across devices.

Content you create. Scripts, series information, and videos you generate or upload in the Studio are stored so we can publish and play them as you direct.

Purchases. Subscriptions and credit packs are processed by Apple. We receive purchase confirmations through our payments partner (RevenueCat) so the app can unlock memberships and credit balances. We never see or store your payment card details.

Usage and device data. We store your in-app settings (such as your monthly credit-spend limit), watch-related preferences, and basic technical logs needed to run and secure the service.

How we use information

To provide the app: sign-in, playback, memberships, credit balances, and the creator studio. To keep the community safe: all published series pass moderation, and users can report content and block creators. To communicate about your account when necessary.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for third-party advertising, and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers that run the app (hosting, payments confirmation, and video generation providers that process the content you ask us to create), and when required by law.

Your choices

You can change your display name and spend limit in the app at any time. To delete your account and associated data, contact us at info@annizon.com and we will delete it, except where retention is required by law.

Children

Reel Drama AI is not directed to children under 13, and accounts should not be created for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
