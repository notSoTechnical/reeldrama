How ReelDrama works

Traditional microdrama is shot live-action at $150K–$300K per series. Our pipeline replaces the shoot, not the storyteller.

1 · Story first

Human-written hooks and cliffhangers, one per episode. Every episode ends on a reason to tap "next."

2 · AI production

Locked character sheets keep faces and worlds consistent across 80 episodes. Scenes, voices, captions and both language versions are generated by the pipeline.

3 · Free everywhere

First episodes premiere free on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, where the audience already lives.

4 · Our own paywall

Full series unlock here — episode coins or unlimited at $19.99/month. We own the audience and the margin.
