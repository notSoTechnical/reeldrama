How ReelDrama works

Traditional microdrama is shot live-action at $150K–$300K per series. Our pipeline replaces the shoot, not the storyteller.

1 · Story first

Human-written hooks and cliffhangers, one per episode. Every episode ends on a reason to tap "next."

2 · AI production

Locked character sheets keep faces and worlds consistent across 80 episodes. Scenes, voices, captions and both language versions are generated by the pipeline.

3 · Free everywhere

First episodes premiere free on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, where the audience already lives.

4 · Our own paywall

Full series unlock here — episode coins or unlimited at $19.99/month. We own the audience and the margin.

For creators — your story, produced

You have the story. Shooting it live-action would cost $150K–$300K. On ReelDrama, our AI production pipeline turns your script into a finished vertical series in days — and puts it in front of a paying audience, in English and Chinese.

1 · Submit your story

A script or an outline is enough. You keep ownership of your IP — always.

2 · You produce it — instantly

Type your story, and the pipeline builds it: locked characters, voices in both languages, subtitles, cliffhangers. You approve every episode before it goes live. Nothing publishes without your yes.

3 · It premieres everywhere

Free episodes on TikTok, Reels and Shorts build the audience. Connect your own YouTube channel and finished episodes can publish there automatically — the moment your series is done, it is live. The full series also lives behind the ReelDrama paywall.

4 · You earn 70%

Creators keep 70% of their series' paywall revenue. ReelDrama keeps 30% for production, distribution and payments. No upfront cost during the invite phase.
